Our Team

Meet the dedicated professionals who make DHI India's mission possible. Our team brings together expertise from both Bhutan and India.

A Team Built on Excellence

Our team comprises professionals with diverse backgrounds in finance, operations, logistics, and international trade. Together, we work towards building a seamless economic bridge between Bhutan and India.

With expertise spanning both markets, our team understands the unique challenges and opportunities of cross-border business, enabling us to deliver exceptional value to our stakeholders.

We are always looking for talented individuals who share our vision of strengthening Bhutan-India economic ties.
