Corporate Governance

Our governance framework ensures transparency, accountability, and ethical business practices in all our operations.

Governance Framework

DHI India is committed to maintaining the highest standards of corporate governance. Our governance structure is designed to ensure transparency, accountability, and ethical business practices in all our operations.

As a joint venture between Druk Holding & Investments (Bhutan) and Kusum Group (India), we adhere to the governance standards of both jurisdictions while maintaining our commitment to international best practices.

Our Board of Directors provides strategic oversight and guidance, ensuring that all decisions are made in the best interests of our stakeholders and in alignment with our core values.

Our Governance Pillars

01

Transparency

We maintain open communication with all stakeholders and ensure timely disclosure of material information.

02

Accountability

Clear roles and responsibilities ensure that decision-makers are accountable for their actions.

03

Integrity

We conduct all business with the highest ethical standards and zero tolerance for corruption.

Board Structure

Our Board comprises representatives from both promoter entities and independent directors, ensuring balanced decision-making and diverse perspectives.

Board Composition

Representatives from DHI and Kusum Group with independent directors

Committees

Audit, Risk, and Investment committees for specialized oversight

Meetings

Regular board meetings with structured agenda and documentation

Regulatory Compliance

Tax Compliance
