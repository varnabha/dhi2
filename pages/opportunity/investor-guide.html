Investment Opportunities

DHI India offers various investment opportunities for institutional and individual investors looking to participate in the growth of the Bhutan-India economic corridor.

Our investment approach focuses on sectors with strong fundamentals, sustainable growth potential, and alignment with bilateral economic interests.

Investment Criteria

01

Sector Alignment

Investments aligned with our core focus areas: infrastructure, energy, minerals, and enterprise.

02

Growth Potential

Target companies with demonstrated growth potential and scalable business models.

03

Strategic Fit

Opportunities that enhance our portfolio synergy and cross-border capabilities.
